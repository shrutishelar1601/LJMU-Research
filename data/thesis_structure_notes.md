# Thesis Sample Structure Notes

Source: `/Users/anuraagd/Downloads/research/M.S Thesis Sample Document.docx`

## What the sample is

This file is an instruction sheet, not a completed thesis. It contains no research findings, no dataset, no figures, and no references. It should be used as a formatting and chapter-organization guide only.

## Required structure extracted from the sample

1. Cover page
   - Research title
   - Learner name
   - Month and year at the bottom
2. Abstract
   - Approximately 250 words
   - Background, scope, problem, gap, aim/objective, proposed method, novelty, validation, overall analysis
3. Keywords
   - Six keywords
4. Chapter 1: Introduction
   - No subheadings
   - Approximately 2–3 pages
   - Technical flow from background to scope, problem, gap, and objectives
   - Minimum approximately 25 recent references in the introduction according to the template
5. Chapter 2: Literature Review
   - 25–30 additional papers, without duplicating the introduction
   - Preferably 2021 onward, with authoritative older foundations where necessary
   - Separate paragraph for each study
   - Explain approach, novelty, and reported results
   - Technical gaps subsection of approximately 20 lines
6. Chapter 3: Proposed Methodology
   - Materials
   - Dataset
   - Data preprocessing
   - Feature extraction
   - Data augmentation, only if scientifically justified
   - Other dataset operations
   - Methods and mathematical theory
   - Named proposed methodology
   - Problem definition
   - System model
   - Architecture and working
   - Algorithm and flowchart
7. Chapter 4: Implementation, Results and Analysis
   - Experimental setup
   - Hardware/software versions
   - Performance metrics with equations
   - Results for each metric with tables and graphs
   - Analysis
   - Limitations
   - Ablation study
   - Comparison with state-of-the-art methods
8. Chapter 5: Conclusion and Future Scope
   - What was proposed
   - Novelty
   - Actual experimental findings
   - Limitations
   - Future technical work
9. References
   - Target 55–90 references
   - Harvard-style author-date citations are requested by the proposal, while the sample also asks for numbering; this must be resolved into one consistent convention before formatting.

## Scientific corrections required for this project

- The sample's instruction to report percentages and experimental results cannot be followed until real experiments are completed.
- No projected values from the proposal may be presented as measured results.
- Synthetic or weakly sourced Kaggle data cannot be described as clinical evidence.
- A dataset's public download status does not automatically grant permission for formal research; licence, repository terms, ethics, and provenance must be recorded.
- The paper must distinguish development, internal evaluation, external evaluation, exploratory analysis, and literature-reported results.
- For a prediction-model thesis, TRIPOD+AI should be used for reporting and PROBAST+AI for risk-of-bias/applicability appraisal.
- The final chapter structure can be retained even if the empirical scope changes; the title, questions, outcome, dataset, and result claims must change together.

## Page-budget implication for an 80–90-page document

The sample does not define exact page counts. A defensible working allocation is:

- Front matter, abstract, lists, abbreviations: 5–8 pages
- Chapter 1: 3–5 pages
- Chapter 2: 18–25 pages
- Chapter 3: 20–25 pages
- Chapter 4: 20–25 pages
- Chapter 5: 3–5 pages
- References: 8–12 pages
- Appendices: additional pages as needed

The page budget should be met through substantive literature synthesis, equations, data dictionaries, algorithms, reproducibility details, results, figures, and appendices—not by expanding paragraphs or inventing results.
